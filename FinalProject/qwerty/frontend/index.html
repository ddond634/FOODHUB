About MealHub

Affordable, sustainable meals with smart features for consumers and local vendors.

Project Focus

MealHub offers meals, food products, and beverages at budget-friendly prices with options for individuals and families.

  • Budget meals, combo meals, and meal plans
  • Product listings for meals, beverages, bundles
  • Simple cart and secure checkout
  • Search and filters by diet and price
  • Seller dashboard for local vendors

SDG Alignment

  • SDG 2 – End Hunger: connects consumers to affordable, nutritious meals and supports small local providers.
  • SDG 12 – Responsible Consumption: promotes eco‑friendly packaging, portion control, and awareness to reduce waste.

Creativity & Innovation

  • Smart recommendations for combos and sustainable picks
  • Zero‑Waste deals for near‑expiry surplus
  • Community tips on sustainable eating
  • Vendor verification for responsible production
